<details>
<summary>why this painting</summary>

The rebel angels didn't accept the system as given. They questioned it, broke from it, and fell for it — which is a reasonable price for refusing defaults.

Luca Giordano was nicknamed **"Luca fa presto"** — Luca works fast. Commissioned by kings, painted entire cathedral ceilings in months, never slowed down. The painter and the painting are the same idea: refuse the first draft, move fast, make it matter.

Michael above isn't reckless. He's methodical, calm, completely certain. That's the part that interests me.

*La Chute des Anges Rebelles, oil on canvas, c. 1666 — Museo del Prado, Madrid*
